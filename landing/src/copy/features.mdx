## Stability

Bowerbird is still in an alpha state.

Bowerbird never deletes your original photos; it only ever moves photos between folders in the library.

However, the catalogue which holds your albums, picks, and edits, is still changing. Updates may be unstable and may corrupt catalogues. Your photos will not be lost, but any settings, triage data or photo edits may be lost.

The renderer is also still being actively improved, so updates may change how rendered photos look.

</div>
<div>

## Requirements

- At minimum, viewing and triage need a device with a web browser.
- We recommend using the desktop app, but you can edit RAWs on most devices and browsers.
- Bowerbird uses up to 4 GB of system memory and 4 GB of graphics memory.
