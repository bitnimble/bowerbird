## Stability

Bowerbird is in alpha. It never deletes your originals, and only moves files between folders in the library.

The catalogue that holds your albums, Picks, and edits is still changing. Updates may corrupt it. Your originals won't be lost, but your settings, triage verdicts, or edits may be.

Photo processing is also changing, so updates may change how edited photos look.

</div>
<div>

## Requirements

- Viewing and triage need a device with a web browser.
- We recommend the desktop app, though you can edit RAWs on most devices and browsers.
- Bowerbird uses up to 4 GB of system memory and 4 GB of graphics memory.
